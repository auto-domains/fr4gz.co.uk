## What this site is

{{ site.domain }} is an independent fan site about **fragging**: what the word means, where it came from, the slang that grew up around it and the frag movies and esports plays that show it at its best.

## How videos are chosen

The [videos page](/videos/) collects well-known frag movies, famous tournament plays and documentaries about first-person shooter history. Official tournament and publisher channels are preferred where they exist, because their uploads tend to stay online. Classic frag movies from the Quake and Unreal Tournament era are usually only available from fan channels.

Every video is embedded from YouTube and belongs to its creator. Videos were last checked as available on {{ site.videosChecked | readableDate }}. If one has been removed, please [let us know](/contact/).
